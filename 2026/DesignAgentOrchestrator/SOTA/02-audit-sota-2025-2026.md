# Audit SOTA 2025–2026

This file focuses on the reliability, bias, and failure-mode literature that is most relevant to making audits trustworthy rather than merely automated.

## 1. Language bias in LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation
- arXiv: [2607.14480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14480)
- PDF: [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.14480)
- Summary: This line of work highlights how evaluation quality can be distorted by linguistic framing or language-specific priors in judge prompts.
- Why it matters here: Any audit pipeline in DesignAgentOrchestrator should be robust to prompt framing and multilingual variation.

## 2. Eval-Pair Matrix
- arXiv: [2607.10626](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10626)
- PDF: [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.10626)
- Summary: The paper studies structured pairwise evaluation in a way that can expose where judge systems disagree and where confidence is overestimated.
- Why it matters here: It is a useful conceptual model for comparing multiple agents or multiple critique passes before accepting a result.

## 3. Closed-loop unreliability in judge pipelines
- arXiv: [2607.13347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13347)
- PDF: [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.13347)
- Summary: This work stresses that judge models can become self-reinforcingly unreliable when the evaluation loop is not controlled.
- Why it matters here: It reinforces the need for explicit guardrails, fallback judges, and audit trails in an orchestrator.

## 4. Cheating audit pipeline for agentic evaluation
- arXiv: [2607.21763](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21763)
- PDF: [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.21763)
- Summary: The paper proposes an audit-oriented approach to detect evaluation shortcuts, gaming, or reward hacking behavior in agentic systems.
- Why it matters here: It is directly relevant if DesignAgentOrchestrator must inspect not only outputs but also the trajectory of agent actions.

## 5. Security debt audit for agent systems
- arXiv: [2607.12428](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12428)
- PDF: [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.12428)
- Summary: This work frames security and compliance debt as something that must be audited progressively rather than only at deployment time.
- Why it matters here: It supports the idea that auditability should be part of the orchestration architecture from the start.

## Related baselines worth tracking

- RewardBench and JudgeBench for benchmark-style judge comparison.
- Prometheus-2 as a domain-specific evaluation model.
- Self-preference bias research as a caution against naïve single-model judging.
